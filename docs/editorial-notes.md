# Editorial notes · 编选记录

For the poems themselves, start with the [seven-grid README selection](../README.md) or [all 18 grids](../more_poems.md). These notes retain the editorial reasoning, reservations, and AI review history.

## Reading rules and selection

Collection 1 contains rhyming poem-pairs: rows left to right, taken top to bottom, form one poem; columns top to bottom, taken left to right, form the other. All five line endings must rhyme in each reading. Backwards and diagonal readings are not required.

Collection 2 preserves the earlier, unrhymed forms and their new additions. Same-poem squares, reversible grids, and unrestricted forward pairs are kept in separate ranked groups. This retains both the symmetric and reversible kinds from the older experiments without conflating their reading rules.

Every grid has five rows of five simplified Chinese characters. Punctuation belongs to the displayed readings, not the 25-cell grid. A written 儿 counts as one character even when pronounced as erhua.

Ranks are editorial judgments within a form, not numerical measurements. The order prioritizes the weaker reading’s language and whole-poem coherence, then memorable specificity and the change of meaning achieved by the grid. Mechanical correctness is a gate, not an aesthetic score. Selected means recommended now; Reserve means readable but secondary; Workshop means a useful experiment with unresolved weaknesses.

Rhyme uses the modern Mandarin families in [《中华通韵》, GF 0022—2019](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/202111/W020211118492193544846.pdf). Different medials can belong to one family; 四衣 also groups i and the apical-vowel readings written i. No classical tone-pattern claim is made.

The selected rhyming edition now contains five grids: new 归去、绣愁、留饮 alongside the earlier 归舟、暮归. No earlier grid has been overwritten. The earlier same-poem and reversible selections remain in Collection 2, clearly separated from the all-line-rhyming requirement.

### 1.《归去》 — Selected

**New this round** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
人 归 旧 柳 寒
去 鸟 枕 青 烟
旧 枕 重 帘 掩
柳 青 帘 外 晚
寒 烟 掩 晚 山
```

**Across →**

> 人归旧柳寒，  
> 去鸟枕青烟。  
> 旧枕重帘掩，  
> 柳青，帘外晚，  
> 寒烟掩晚山。  

**Down ↓**

> 人去旧柳寒，  
> 归鸟枕青烟。  
> 旧枕重帘掩，  
> 柳青，帘外晚，  
> 寒烟掩晚山。  

**Rhyme:** 十一安 · an / ian / uan / üan · tones unrestricted.

→ 寒 hán · 烟 yān · 掩 yǎn · 晚 wǎn · 山 shān

↓ 寒 hán · 烟 yān · 掩 yǎn · 晚 wǎn · 山 shān

**Poem note:** The person returns while the bird leaves; turn the square and the person leaves while the bird returns. The unchanged pillow and curtains make the exchange matter. Three shared lines act as a fixed scene against which presence and absence change.

**Why here:** The smallest change produces the strongest new atmospheric contrast: 人归／去鸟 becomes 人去／归鸟. The old pillow, curtains and distant hills stay fixed while the human presence changes. The literal pillow gives the bird’s figurative pillow a bodily counterpart; the repeated 掩 connects the room with the veiled mountains. Both external readers selected it, and the fresh comparative reader ranked it first.

**Limit:** 去鸟枕青烟 combines departure with repose more daringly than 归鸟枕青烟. Three full lines are shared; this is a restrained change of perspective, not two independent stories.

**Reading:** 枕青烟 is a visual metaphor, not a claim that birds literally sleep on mist. 旧枕重帘掩 places the old pillow behind layered curtains. 柳青／帘外晚 juxtaposes the green willow and dusk outside the curtain. No bereavement, abandoned marriage, or subsequent bird landing is specified.


**Across →**

> 舟隐村外烟  
> 客路水云漫  
> 梦随云渐远  
> 寒山连远天  

**Down ↓**

> 归舟客梦寒  
> 帆隐路随山  
> 过村水云连  
> 渚外云渐远  
> 晚烟漫远天  

**Rhyme:** 十一安 · an / ian / uan / üan · tones unrestricted.

→ 晚 wǎn · 烟 yān · 漫 màn · 远 yuǎn · 天 tiān

↓ 寒 hán · 山 shān · 连 lián · 远 yuǎn · 天 tiān

**Why here:** Still the most even earlier pair. Observation opens into dream across; the cold dream frames an outward view down. The new 归去 leads for specificity and the significance of its changed human presence, while 归舟 remains smoother than the more conceptually ambitious 绣愁.

**Limit:** 帆隐路随山 is the weakest seam: 帆隐／路随山 changes subject abruptly. 客路水云漫 supplies atmosphere more than a precise observation.

**Reading:** Across: a returning sail, village haze, water and clouds, a receding dream, distant mountains. Down: a cold traveler’s dream opens outward into sails, route, village, clouds and evening haze. The text does not say the traveler changes to a land route.

### 3.《绣愁》 — Selected

**New this round** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
相 对 方 念 旧
看 图 识 旧 友
故 问 愁 谁 有
人 老 难 如 旧
瘦 手 绣 旧 愁
```

**Across →**

> 相对方念旧，  
> 看图识旧友。  
> 故问：愁谁有？  
> 人老难如旧，  
> 瘦手绣旧愁。  

**Down ↓**

> 相看故人瘦，  
> 对图问老手，  
> 方识愁难绣。  
> 念旧，谁如旧？  
> 旧友有旧愁。  

**Rhyme:** 十欧 · ou / iou · tones unrestricted.

→ 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu · 有 yǒu · 旧 jiù · 愁 chóu

↓ 瘦 shòu · 手 shǒu · 绣 xiù · 旧 jiù · 愁 chóu

**Poem note:** Recognizing an old friend in an image becomes recognizing the limits of an art. The thin hand nevertheless stitches the old sorrow. The two readings hold effort and difficulty together; they do not cancel each other out.

**Why here:** The most substantial new change of meaning: 识 moves from recognizing an old friend in an image to understanding the difficulty of expressing sorrow. 瘦手绣旧愁 gives that difficulty a bodily, material answer. 人老难如旧 and 念旧谁如旧 connect remembered likeness with changed bodies. The editor and one cross-reader selected it; the fresh comparative reader preferred Reserve because its connective lines are less effortless. It ranks below 归舟 for evenness, above 留饮 for the force of its central transformation.

**Limit:** 故问愁谁有 is the loosest connection and uses literary question-word inversion. 对图问老手 is functional rather than especially lyrical. Four occurrences of 旧 unify the subject but also reveal the construction.

**Reading:** The image contains a recognizable old friend; the vertical reading asks an experienced hand about the image and discovers sorrow is difficult to embroider. 老手 means an experienced practitioner, not necessarily the same person as the thin friend. The text does not establish a photograph rather than another image, an actual reunion, or anyone’s gender. 难绣 means difficult, not impossible.

**Revision record:** Collaborative work, not an independent one-shot: the fresh author supplied the embroidery scene and both rhyming borders; the editor completed the square using part of the earlier 旧楼 scaffold; the author refined 唯/有/忘 to 方/识/如. Earlier 旧楼 remains unchanged.

### 4.《留饮》 — Selected

**New this round** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
临 风 谁 劝 酒
窗 寒 人 醉 后
灯 火 与 君 留
影 暖 我 如 旧
旧 酒 酬 旧 友
```

**Across →**

> 临风谁劝酒？  
> 窗寒，人醉后，  
> 灯火与君留，  
> 影暖我如旧。  
> 旧酒酬旧友。  

**Down ↓**

> 临窗灯影旧，  
> 风寒，火暖酒。  
> 谁人与我酬？  
> 劝醉君如旧，  
> 酒后留旧友。  

**Rhyme:** 十欧 · ou / iou · tones unrestricted.

→ 酒 jiǔ · 后 hòu · 留 liú · 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu

↓ 旧 jiù · 酒 jiǔ · 酬 chóu · 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu

**Poem note:** Across, lamplight and the familiar shadow soften the cold window; down, warming wine becomes exchanging toasts and keeping a friend a little longer. The same room supports two connected movements rather than two unrelated collections of images.

**Why here:** Both readings sustain the same hospitable interior but organize it differently. Across, the cold window gives way to lamplight, emotional warmth and wine offered to a friend. Down, the familiar light leads through warming wine and exchanging toasts to asking the friend to stay. Replacing 且 with 火 made both the lamp line and the wine-warming line more concrete; two independent cross-readers accepted the revised pair.

**Limit:** 谁人与我酬 and 劝醉君如旧 are distinctly literary. 影暖我如旧 transfers emotional warmth to a shadow; the preceding 灯火 supports it, but it remains a conspicuous metaphor.

**Reading:** Read 风寒／火暖酒 as two clauses: the wind is cold; the fire warms the wine. 酬 has its wine-toasting sense, supported by the surrounding 酒、醉、友. The opening question does not establish an absent or dead friend.

**Revision record:** Independent draft by rhyme_scene; the editor changed 且 to 火 at (3,2), improving 灯火与君留 and 风寒火暖酒 together. Earlier 伴 at the central cell was revised by the author to 与.

Original pre-repair grid, retained for the record:

```text
临风谁劝酒
窗寒人醉后
灯且与君留
影暖我如旧
旧酒酬旧友
```

### 5.《暮归》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
孤 舟 向 暮 山
舟 中 风 雨 寒
向 风 望 归 雁
暮 雨 归 家 难

```

**Across →**

> 孤舟向暮山  
> 舟中风雨寒  
> 向风望归雁  
> 暮雨归家难  
> 滩寒帆晚还  

**Down ↓**

> 孤舟向暮滩  
> 舟中风雨寒  
> 向风望归帆  
> 暮雨归家晚  
> 山寒雁难还  

**Rhyme:** 十一安 · an / ian / uan / üan · tones unrestricted.

→ 山 shān · 寒 hán · 雁 yàn · 难 nán · 还 huán

↓ 滩 tān · 寒 hán · 帆 fān · 晚 wǎn · 还 huán

**Why here:** Retained intact from the earlier leading pair. Watching geese becomes watching sails, and the concluding difficulty shifts from human homecoming to the geese. It remains coherent and readable; the broader set now offers more varied and less explicit treatments of return, friendship and memory.

**Limit:** 滩寒帆晚还 is compressed and partly repeats the preceding cold and delay; 暮滩 is less settled diction than 暮山. The two poems retain very similar syntax.

**Reading:** 难 is nán, difficult; 还 is huán, return. A difficult homecoming followed by a late return is not a contradiction. 雁难还 does not mean return is impossible.

### 6.《旧楼》 — Reserve

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
相 对 都 念 旧
看 图 说 旧 友
笑 问 楼 谁 守
如 故 不 嫌 旧
旧 友 修 旧 楼
```

**Across →**

> 相对都念旧  
> 看图说旧友  
> 笑问楼谁守  
> 如故不嫌旧  
> 旧友修旧楼  

**Down ↓**

> 相看笑如旧  
> 对图问故友  
> 都说楼不修  
> 念旧谁嫌旧  
> 旧友守旧楼  

**Rhyme:** 十欧 · ou / iou · tones unrestricted.

→ 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu · 守 shǒu · 旧 jiù · 楼 lóu

↓ 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu · 修 xiū · 旧 jiù · 楼 lóu

**Why here:** The old friends, image and building form an identifiable human situation. Repairing across versus guarding an unrepaired building down gives the form a factual consequence. Its repetitive wording keeps it below the selected set. Its original grid is preserved even though part of its syntax informed the later embroidery pair.

**Limit:** 如故不嫌旧 has an indistinct attachment, and 旧 is overworked. The vertical poem says the building is not or will not be repaired; it does not explain why.

**Reading:** Do not turn 不修 into opposition to demolition, historically sensitive restoration, or secret repair. Those stories are not in the words.

*Collection title; originally labelled Old friends and an old building.*

### 7.《江边窗光》 — Workshop

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
江 光 浮 西 墙
风 在 江 面 上
拂 水 帆 影 晃
南 面 船 临 窗
墙 上 晃 窗 光
```

**Across →**

> 江光浮西墙  
> 风在江面上  
> 拂水帆影晃  
> 南面船临窗  
> 墙上晃窗光  

**Down ↓**

> 江风拂南墙  
> 光在水面上  
> 浮江帆船晃  
> 西面影临窗  
> 墙上晃窗光  

**Rhyme:** 十三昂 · ang / iang / uang · tones unrestricted.

→ 墙 qiáng · 上 shàng · 晃 huàng · 窗 chuāng · 光 guāng

↓ 墙 qiáng · 上 shàng · 晃 huàng · 窗 chuāng · 光 guāng

**Why here:** The revised scene no longer needs an invented paper painting. Breeze, river light, sails, walls and windows belong to one physical setting. That stability puts it slightly above 寄知己 in the present ranking.

**Limit:** 浮江帆船晃 compresses a modifier awkwardly; 西面影临窗 leaves the shadow vague. Compass directions and repeated light descriptions still feel dictated by the crossings.

**Reading:** Across starts from river light on a wall and passes through breeze and sail reflection; down starts from wind against a wall and moves through water light and a swaying boat. These are rearranged observations, not sharply different emotional conclusions.

### 8.《寄知己》 — Workshop

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
我 为 谁 写 诗
因 君 有 此 意
你 留 诗 待 寄
写 此 谁 人 知
此 笔 寄 知 己
```

**Across →**

> 我为谁写诗？  
> 因君有此意。  
> 你留诗待寄，  
> 写此谁人知？  
> 此笔寄知己。  

**Down ↓**

> 我因你写此，  
> 为君留此笔。  
> 谁有诗谁寄；  
> 写此待人知，  
> 诗意寄知己。  

**Rhyme:** 四衣 · i / -i · tones unrestricted.

→ 诗 shī · 意 yì · 寄 jì · 知 zhī · 己 jǐ

↓ 此 cǐ · 笔 bǐ · 寄 jì · 知 zhī · 己 jǐ

**Why here:** The final gift changes from the physical 此笔 to intangible 诗意. That is a worthwhile device, and both readings pass the formal checks. Independent review nevertheless found the whole expression less secure than its individual phrases.

**Limit:** 你留诗待寄 changes who holds and sends the poem without connecting the roles clearly. 谁有诗谁寄 is grammatical but abruptly becomes a general maxim. Do not invent reciprocal poetry exchanges or a mail-carrying arrangement to fill those gaps.

**Reading:** Across asks about purpose and recognition, ending with a pen sent to a confidant. Down states a cause for writing, keeps a pen for the addressee, and ends by sending poetic feeling. This remains an experiment, not a promoted finished pair.

**Revision record:** Author's final revision includes the parent's suggested 谁→待 at row 3 column 4; the author then independently replaced two characters to make the pen literal.

*Collection title.*

### 9.《姐姐》 — Workshop

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
姐 姐 逗 我 乐
姐 姐 我 饿 了
笑 我 够 了 么
我 怕 了 姐 姐
饿 了 么 姐 姐
```

**Across →**

> 姐姐逗我乐。  
> 姐姐，我饿了。  
> 笑我，够了么？  
> 我怕了姐姐。  
> 饿了么，姐姐？  

**Down ↓**

> 姐姐笑我饿。  
> 姐姐，我怕了。  
> 逗我，够了么？  
> 我饿了，姐姐。  
> 乐了么，姐姐？  

**Rhyme:** 三鹅 · e / ie / üe · tones unrestricted.

→ 乐 lè · 了 le · 么 me · 姐 jiě · 姐 jiě

↓ 饿 è · 了 le · 么 me · 姐 jiě · 姐 jiě

**Rhyme limitation:** The displayed pinyin records the intended me. For interrogative 么 used like 吗, [汉典 lists ma](https://zdic.net/hans/%E4%B9%88), separately from its me uses. With ma, both third-line endings fall outside 三鹅. A deliberate me reading fits the pattern, but this is not a robust ordinary-pronunciation pass; it is excluded from strict selection.

**Why here:** The childlike complaint is more individual than many landscape grids. 逗我乐 becomes 笑我饿, and the vertical poem develops a recognizable protest. Its low strict-selection rank reflects an unresolved rhyme issue as well as the weaker horizontal ending, not a claim that colloquial writing is inferior.

**Limit:** 饿了么姐姐 suddenly asks about the sister’s hunger after the speaker’s hunger and fear. A charitable reader must not invent a tender reconciliation. Repeated 姐姐 and grammatical particles also carry much of the rhyme.

**Reading:** The final vertical question can sound reproachful: asking whether the sister has had her fun. Sarcasm is an interpretation; a specific abusive incident or reconciliation is not stated.

**Revision record:** Uses the author's proposed center-character repair 错→够. Original third lines were 笑我错了么 / 逗我错了么; all other characters unchanged.

Original pre-repair grid, retained for the record:

```text
姐姐逗我乐
姐姐我饿了
笑我错了么
我怕了姐姐
饿了么姐姐
```

*Collection title; author called this a near-miss.*

#### 1.《认人》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
奶 奶 又 叫 妈
奶 奶 认 出 我
又 认 错 一 回
叫 出 一 声 来
妈 我 回 来 了
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 奶奶又叫“妈”。  
> 奶奶认出我，  
> 又认错一回。  
> 叫出一声来：  
> “妈，我回来了。”  

**Why here:** The neighboring 认出 and 认错 briefly offer recognition, then withdraw it; the closing quoted address makes that correction legible. Its specificity and concentrated turn narrowly outweigh 留一间’s smoother, more general reassurance.

**Limit:** 叫出一声来 is mainly a bridge into the quotation. 认出→认错 is an intentional correction or irony, not two simultaneously literal claims of correct identification.

**Reading:** The last line is read as the grandmother’s speech: 妈，我回来了. The poem does not establish a diagnosis, whether her mother is alive, or an unstated family history.

#### 2.《留一间》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
我 说 家 还 在
说 屋 里 有 人
家 里 留 一 间
还 有 一 人 等
在 人 间 等 你
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 我说家还在  
> 说屋里有人  
> 家里留一间  
> 还有一人等  
> 在人间等你  

**Why here:** Home narrows into a room and a waiting person before 人间 expands the final promise. The contextual omission of 房 in 留一间 is ordinary, not a repair. It remains an excellent companion to the more sharply particular 认人.

**Limit:** 说屋里有人 is mostly preparatory and repeats the reassurance. The scene is less individual than the top-ranked entry.

**Reading:** An available room and someone waiting are explicit. Death or permanent separation is not.

#### 3.《想听你笑》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
我 想 听 你 笑
想 起 你 和 我
听 你 骂 我 傻
你 和 我 一 样
笑 我 傻 样 儿
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 我想听你笑  
> 想起你和我  
> 听你骂我傻  
> 你和我一样  
> 笑我傻样儿  

**Why here:** The wish to hear laughter develops into remembered teasing. The mutuality of 你和我 suits the symmetric form, and the colloquial voice distinguishes it from the more conventional 候归.

**Limit:** 笑我傻样儿 is clipped and regionally marked. 想起你和我 is broad rather than concrete. These are limits of register and precision, not automatic grammatical failures.

**Reading:** Laughter supports an affectionate reading of 骂, without making it the only possible reading. 儿 occupies one written cell even if pronounced as erhua.


#### 1.《留客》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · Four cardinal readings · → ↓ ← ↑

```text
山 深 归 客 远
寒 夜 人 留 客
归 人 等 人 归
客 留 人 夜 寒
远 客 归 深 山
```

**Across →**

> 山深，归客远。  
> 寒夜，人留客。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 客留人，夜寒。  
> 远客归深山。  

**Down ↓**

> 山寒，归客远。  
> 深夜，人留客。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 客留人，夜深。  
> 远客归寒山。  

**Reversed rows ←**

> 远客归深山。  
> 客留人，夜寒。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 寒夜，人留客。  
> 山深，归客远。  

**Reversed columns ↑**

> 远客归寒山。  
> 客留人，夜深。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 深夜，人留客。  
> 山寒，归客远。  

**Why here:** 归人等人归 is the strongest central line in this group: being homeward-bound does not end waiting. 人留客 and 客留人 reverse the relation between host and guest. All four orders remain intelligible.

**Limit:** The other person in 客留人 is unspecified. 深/寒 alters atmosphere more than fundamental meaning. Reversal reorders existing lines; there are nine distinct line strings, not twenty new ones.

**Reading:** The forward readings end with movement toward mountains; the reverse readings end with the returning traveler distant. 归人 does not establish that someone has already arrived.

#### 2.《长巷》 — Reserve

**Earlier entry** · Four cardinal readings · → ↓ ← ↑

```text
长 夜 人 归 晚
巷 静 来 客 归
人 思 又 思 人
归 客 来 静 巷
晚 归 人 夜 长
```

**Across →**

> 长夜人归晚  
> 巷静来客归  
> 人思又思人  
> 归客来静巷  
> 晚归人夜长  

**Down ↓**

> 长巷人归晚  
> 夜静思客归  
> 人来又来人  
> 归客思静夜  
> 晚归人巷长  

**Reversed rows ←**

> 晚归人夜长  
> 归客来静巷  
> 人思又思人  
> 巷静来客归  
> 长夜人归晚  

**Reversed columns ↑**

> 晚归人巷长  
> 归客思静夜  
> 人来又来人  
> 夜静思客归  
> 长巷人归晚  

**Why here:** The exchange between 长夜/夜长 and 长巷/巷长 gives time and distance a useful relation. It ranks ahead of 未完的对话 here because the weakest reading is smoother and the setting more concrete.

**Limit:** 人思又思人 and 人来又来人 show the repetitive mechanism. Its reversals are reordered forward lines rather than four independently new sets of lines.

**Reading:** 归客思静夜 means the returning traveler thinks of or longs for a quiet night; it does not explicitly mean resting during a quiet night.

#### 3.《未完的对话》 — Workshop

**Earlier entry** · Four cardinal readings · → ↓ ← ↑

```text
我 等 你 问 风
听 雨 听 山 问
你 问 不 听 我
等 风 问 雨 等
风 听 我 等 你
```

**Across →**

> 我等，你问风。  
> 听雨，听山，问。  
> 你问，不听我。  
> 等风，问雨，等。  
> 风听，我等你。  

**Down ↓**

> 我听，你等风。  
> 等雨，问风，听。  
> 你听，不问我。  
> 问山，听雨，等。  
> 风问，我等你。  

**Reversed rows ←**

> 风问，你等我。  
> 问山，听雨，听。  
> 我听，不问你。  
> 等雨，问风，等。  
> 你等，我听风。  

**Reversed columns ↑**

> 风等，你听我。  
> 听风，问雨，等。  
> 我问，不听你。  
> 等雨，听山，问。  
> 你等，我问风。  

**Why here:** As punctuated free verse, the changes between asking without listening and listening without asking genuinely alter the human relation. One peer narrowly preferred it to 长巷 for that dramatic effect. The present general-reading order gives fluency greater weight.

**Limit:** Sequences such as 听雨，听山，问 end in arranged-sounding one-word commands. The twenty distinct strings are not twenty equally strong lines. Punctuation is essential.

**Reading:** Reverse row 5 is 你等，我听风; reverse column 5 is 你等，我问风. Neither reading contains a shared 你我 subject.

#### 1.《入山》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · Two poems, no rhyme requirement · → ↓

```text
微 风 入 竹 林
雨 轻 山 色 深
入 林 心 自 远
花 影 自 留 客
林 深 闲 客 居
```

**Across →**

> 微风入竹林  
> 雨轻山色深  
> 入林心自远  
> 花影自留客  
> 林深闲客居  

**Down ↓**

> 微雨入花林  
> 风轻林影深  
> 入山心自闲  
> 竹色自留客  
> 林深远客居  

**Why here:** Both readings sustain a scene and the visitor’s response. 心自远 becomes 心自闲, and 闲客 becomes 远客. The change is not dramatic, but the Chinese is more settled than 梦中辨认.

**Limit:** Much of the imagery is conventional, and several changes are landscape substitutions rather than a new argument.

**Reading:** 花影自留客 and 竹色自留客 personify the attractions of the setting; no elaborate backstory is required.

#### 2.《梦中辨认》 — Workshop

**Earlier entry** · Two poems, no rhyme requirement · → ↓

```text
我 说 你 仍 在
听 你 说 旧 梦
你 在 我 梦 中
说 梦 非 相 见
梦 里 我 见 你
```

**Across →**

> 我说你仍在  
> 听你说旧梦  
> 你在我梦中  
> 说梦非相见  
> 梦里我见你  

**Down ↓**

> 我听你说梦  
> 说你在梦里  
> 你说我非我  
> 仍旧梦相见  
> 在梦中见你  

**Why here:** Dreamed presence versus an actual meeting becomes a question about the dreamed person’s identity. That is a substantial formal idea worth retaining.

**Limit:** 仍旧梦相见 is conspicuously compressed, and dream/meeting vocabulary repeats without enough further movement.

**Reading:** Dreaming of meeting is the more defensible reading of 梦相见. Do not silently insert 在梦中 to claim that the written syntax is fully effortless.

## Review record

### Current rhyming-refinement round

Three creators began with no inherited conversation, with different briefs: observed scene, intimate human detail, and a consequential change between readings. All three confirmed avoiding shared files and previous poems during independent creation.

This round then used active collaborative revision rather than pretending every final poem emerged independently. The scene writer’s hospitality poem improved from 灯且与君留 to 灯火与君留, also improving the crossing to 风寒火暖酒. The human-detail writer supplied the embroidery scene and rhyming borders; the editor completed its square using part of 旧楼’s syntax; the writer then refined 方、识、如. The bird poem was pared back to the clear human/bird return-departure reversal, retaining three shared lines.

A separate fresh-context reviewer received anonymous grids without provenance or earlier rankings. After revision, that reader ranked 归去 > 归舟 > 留饮 > 绣愁 > 暮归, selecting the first three and preferring Reserve for 绣愁. Cross-readers from other creative tasks independently accepted 归去 and 留饮; another selected 绣愁 above 归舟 for its more substantial change of meaning. Self-revision was kept separate from independent cross-review; no self-evaluation counted as a peer endorsement. The editor’s final order is 归去 > 归舟 > 绣愁 > 留饮 > 暮归: the embroidery poem’s material image and expressive tension outweigh its less fluent connective lines, but not enough to displace 归舟’s evenness. 暮归 is retained from the earlier selection rather than discarded for becoming less novel beside new work.

The lexical check on [酬 in 汉典](https://zdic.net/hans/%E9%85%AC) supports its wine-toasting sense; it does not by itself certify the poem’s literary quality. No forced pronunciation was introduced. The previous workshop pieces were not upgraded to enlarge the selection.

### Previous collection round — retained record

Five creators started with no inherited task conversation. They received different briefs: physical scene, ordinary human dialogue, grammatical transformation, symmetric same-poem writing, and reversible writing. Earlier poems and scores were not supplied during creation. Each creator confirmed avoiding shared-file inspection and existing-poem lookup; general pronunciation/rhyme references and scratch checks of their own text were allowed.

Review then used authors from other categories. No peer reviewed their own submission. Reviewers retained their separate creative context; they are not described as blank-context critics.

The main rhyming reviewer ranked 暮归 ahead of 归舟, valuing the swapped human/bird conclusion. The parent preferred 归舟’s overall movement and weaker-reading fluency. A separate, blind-to-prior-scores tie-break also narrowly preferred 归舟. The final order records a close judgment, not a unanimous or measurable victory.

For reversible grids, the peer preferred 未完的对话 to 长巷 for its more consequential changes of agency. The final order reverses those two for a general-reading collection, prioritizing smoother language. A misquoted line in that review was checked against the actual grid, withdrawn, and corrected before its reasoning was retained.

A pronunciation check also caught the conditional question-particle rhyme in 姐姐. Its initial author's intended me reading was not silently accepted as an unconditional standard-pronunciation pass. Geometry, literary quality, and pronunciation reliability remain separate judgments.
