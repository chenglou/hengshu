# 📜 hengshu

**横竖** · *héngshù*

1. Horizontal and vertical; across and down.
2. Either way; in any case.

A collection of 2D Chinese poems by GPT Astra. Unlike traditional poems, these can be read in various directions.

**横竖有诗: poetry either way.**

## Rhyming pairs

→ and ↓ produce different poems; both rhyme.

### 《归去》

![《归去》：诗歌方阵、英文翻译与阅读方向](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dd7514bb-08a1-4782-b5e2-1234fd3a04eb)

```text
人 归 旧 柳 寒
去 鸟 枕 青 烟
旧 枕 重 帘 掩
柳 青 帘 外 晚
寒 烟 掩 晚 山
```

### 《归舟》

![《归舟》：诗歌方阵、英文翻译与阅读方向](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1c4c106d-0bb0-4189-ae84-7e16444791ab)

## Symmetric squares

→ and ↓ produce the same poem.

![《认人》：诗歌方阵、英文翻译与阅读方向](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d01c5e60-a63c-4528-af14-316c486e6f7f)

```text
又 认 错 一 回
叫 出 一 声 来
妈 我 回 来 了
```

## Omnidirectional poem

Readable in all → ↓ ← ↑ directions.

### 《留客》

![《留客》：诗歌方阵、英文翻译与阅读方向](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b19e4edc-9d72-495c-9ae2-618291202fdb)

```text
山 深 归 客 远
寒 夜 人 留 客
归 人 等 人 归
客 留 人 夜 寒
远 客 归 深 山
```

We designed the rhyming pairs around meaningful bottom and right edges made entirely of rhyming characters, then revised the crossing lines until both readings worked. [Process notes](docs/editorial-notes.md).
