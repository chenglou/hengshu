# 横读竖读 · 双向押韵诗选

5 selected grids · Simplified Chinese · 2026-09-03

Each square contains two five-line poems. Read rows left to right, in top-to-bottom order; read columns top to bottom, in left-to-right order. Every line ends in the stated modern Mandarin rhyme family. Reverse readings and classical tone patterns are not required.

The order is editorial, judged first by the less fluent of the two readings. Earlier selections are preserved alongside new work; workshop drafts are excluded from this reading edition. The [complete collection and review record](collection.md) retains them separately.

## 1.《归去》

New selection.

```text
人 归 旧 柳 寒
去 鸟 枕 青 烟
旧 枕 重 帘 掩
柳 青 帘 外 晚
寒 烟 掩 晚 山
```

| 横读 → | 竖读 ↓ |
|---|---|
| 人归旧柳寒， | 人去旧柳寒， |
| 去鸟枕青烟。 | 归鸟枕青烟。 |
| 旧枕重帘掩， | 旧枕重帘掩， |
| 柳青，帘外晚， | 柳青，帘外晚， |
| 寒烟掩晚山。 | 寒烟掩晚山。 |

**韵脚 · 十一安**

→ 寒 hán · 烟 yān · 掩 yǎn · 晚 wǎn · 山 shān

↓ 寒 hán · 烟 yān · 掩 yǎn · 晚 wǎn · 山 shān

The person returns while the bird leaves; turn the square and the person leaves while the bird returns. The unchanged pillow and curtains make the exchange matter. Three shared lines act as a fixed scene against which presence and absence change.

**Editorial reservation:** 去鸟枕青烟 combines departure with repose more daringly than 归鸟枕青烟. Three full lines are shared; this is a restrained change of perspective, not two independent stories.

## 2.《归舟》

Retained from an earlier round.

```text
归 帆 过 渚 晚
舟 隐 村 外 烟
客 路 水 云 漫
梦 随 云 渐 远
寒 山 连 远 天
```

| 横读 → | 竖读 ↓ |
|---|---|
| 归帆过渚晚 | 归舟客梦寒 |
| 舟隐村外烟 | 帆隐路随山 |
| 客路水云漫 | 过村水云连 |
| 梦随云渐远 | 渚外云渐远 |
| 寒山连远天 | 晚烟漫远天 |

**韵脚 · 十一安**

→ 晚 wǎn · 烟 yān · 漫 màn · 远 yuǎn · 天 tiān

↓ 寒 hán · 山 shān · 连 lián · 远 yuǎn · 天 tiān

Still the most even earlier pair. Observation opens into dream across; the cold dream frames an outward view down. The new 归去 leads for specificity and the significance of its changed human presence, while 归舟 remains smoother than the more conceptually ambitious 绣愁.

**Editorial reservation:** 帆隐路随山 is the weakest seam: 帆隐／路随山 changes subject abruptly. 客路水云漫 supplies atmosphere more than a precise observation.

## 3.《绣愁》

New selection.

```text
相 对 方 念 旧
看 图 识 旧 友
故 问 愁 谁 有
人 老 难 如 旧
瘦 手 绣 旧 愁
```

| 横读 → | 竖读 ↓ |
|---|---|
| 相对方念旧， | 相看故人瘦， |
| 看图识旧友。 | 对图问老手， |
| 故问：愁谁有？ | 方识愁难绣。 |
| 人老难如旧， | 念旧，谁如旧？ |
| 瘦手绣旧愁。 | 旧友有旧愁。 |

**韵脚 · 十欧**

→ 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu · 有 yǒu · 旧 jiù · 愁 chóu

↓ 瘦 shòu · 手 shǒu · 绣 xiù · 旧 jiù · 愁 chóu

Recognizing an old friend in an image becomes recognizing the limits of an art. The thin hand nevertheless stitches the old sorrow. The two readings hold effort and difficulty together; they do not cancel each other out.

**Editorial reservation:** 故问愁谁有 is the loosest connection and uses literary question-word inversion. 对图问老手 is functional rather than especially lyrical. Four occurrences of 旧 unify the subject but also reveal the construction.

## 4.《留饮》

New selection.

```text
临 风 谁 劝 酒
窗 寒 人 醉 后
灯 火 与 君 留
影 暖 我 如 旧
旧 酒 酬 旧 友
```

| 横读 → | 竖读 ↓ |
|---|---|
| 临风谁劝酒？ | 临窗灯影旧， |
| 窗寒，人醉后， | 风寒，火暖酒。 |
| 灯火与君留， | 谁人与我酬？ |
| 影暖我如旧。 | 劝醉君如旧， |
| 旧酒酬旧友。 | 酒后留旧友。 |

**韵脚 · 十欧**

→ 酒 jiǔ · 后 hòu · 留 liú · 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu

↓ 旧 jiù · 酒 jiǔ · 酬 chóu · 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu

Across, lamplight and the familiar shadow soften the cold window; down, warming wine becomes exchanging toasts and keeping a friend a little longer. The same room supports two connected movements rather than two unrelated collections of images.

**Editorial reservation:** 谁人与我酬 and 劝醉君如旧 are distinctly literary. 影暖我如旧 transfers emotional warmth to a shadow; the preceding 灯火 supports it, but it remains a conspicuous metaphor.

## 5.《暮归》

Retained from an earlier round.

```text
孤 舟 向 暮 山
舟 中 风 雨 寒
向 风 望 归 雁
暮 雨 归 家 难
滩 寒 帆 晚 还
```

| 横读 → | 竖读 ↓ |
|---|---|
| 孤舟向暮山 | 孤舟向暮滩 |
| 舟中风雨寒 | 舟中风雨寒 |
| 向风望归雁 | 向风望归帆 |
| 暮雨归家难 | 暮雨归家晚 |
| 滩寒帆晚还 | 山寒雁难还 |

**韵脚 · 十一安**

→ 山 shān · 寒 hán · 雁 yàn · 难 nán · 还 huán

↓ 滩 tān · 寒 hán · 帆 fān · 晚 wǎn · 还 huán

Retained intact from the earlier leading pair. Watching geese becomes watching sails, and the concluding difficulty shifts from human homecoming to the geese. It remains coherent and readable; the broader set now offers more varied and less explicit treatments of return, friendship and memory.

**Editorial reservation:** 滩寒帆晚还 is compressed and partly repeats the preceding cold and delay; 暮滩 is less settled diction than 暮山. The two poems retain very similar syntax.

## Rhyme and text checks

All included grids contain exactly 25 Chinese characters; every displayed column has been recomputed from the square. The two readings are not identical. All ten line endings per grid have checked contextual pronunciations within one family of [《中华通韵》, GF 0022—2019](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/202111/W020211118492193544846.pdf). Tones may differ. Rhyme correctness is separate from the editorial judgment of poetic quality.

For the earlier same-poem squares and reversible poems, see [Collection 2 in the complete collection](collection.md). Their different constraints are not presented as equivalent to this selection.
