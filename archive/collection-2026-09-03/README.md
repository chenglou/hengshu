# 简体五乘五诗集

Ranked collection · 2026-09-03 · 15 entries, including 5 additions.

## Reading rules and selection

Collection 1 contains rhyming poem-pairs: rows left to right, taken top to bottom, form one poem; columns top to bottom, taken left to right, form the other. All five line endings must rhyme in each reading. Backwards and diagonal readings are not required.

Collection 2 preserves the earlier, unrhymed forms and their new additions. Same-poem squares, reversible grids, and unrestricted forward pairs are kept in separate ranked groups. This retains both the symmetric and reversible kinds from the older experiments without conflating their reading rules.

Every grid has five rows of five simplified Chinese characters. Punctuation belongs to the displayed readings, not the 25-cell grid. A written 儿 counts as one character even when pronounced as erhua.

Ranks are editorial judgments within a form, not numerical measurements. The order prioritizes the weaker reading’s language and whole-poem coherence, then memorable specificity and the change of meaning achieved by the grid. Mechanical correctness is a gate, not an aesthetic score. Selected means recommended now; Reserve means readable but secondary; Workshop means a useful experiment with unresolved weaknesses.

Rhyme uses the modern Mandarin families in [《中华通韵》, GF 0022—2019](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/202111/W020211118492193544846.pdf). Different medials can belong to one family; 四衣 also groups i and the apical-vowel readings written i. No classical tone-pattern claim is made.

The strongest additions are 《认人》 in the same-poem group and 《留客》 in the reversible group. The earlier 《归舟》 and 《暮归》 still lead the rhyming pair collection. Five additions were retained, but only two new entries earned selection.

### 1.《归舟》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
归 帆 过 渚 晚
舟 隐 村 外 烟
客 路 水 云 漫
梦 随 云 渐 远
寒 山 连 远 天
```

**Across →**

> 归帆过渚晚  
> 舟隐村外烟  
> 客路水云漫  
> 梦随云渐远  
> 寒山连远天  

**Down ↓**

> 归舟客梦寒  
> 帆隐路随山  
> 过村水云连  
> 渚外云渐远  
> 晚烟漫远天  

**Rhyme:** 十一安 · an / ian / uan / üan · tones unrestricted.

→ 晚 wǎn · 烟 yān · 漫 màn · 远 yuǎn · 天 tiān

↓ 寒 hán · 山 shān · 连 lián · 远 yuǎn · 天 tiān

**Why here:** The two readings reorganize a shared scene into different lyric movements: observation opens into dream across; the cold dream frames an outward view down. Both sustain the scene with relatively little explanatory repair. It narrowly leads 暮归, rather than decisively defeating it.

**Limit:** 帆隐路随山 is the weakest seam: 帆隐／路随山 changes subject abruptly. 客路水云漫 supplies atmosphere more than a precise observation.

**Reading:** Across: a returning sail, village haze, water and clouds, a receding dream, distant mountains. Down: a cold traveler’s dream opens outward into sails, route, village, clouds and evening haze. The text does not say the traveler changes to a land route.

### 2.《暮归》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
孤 舟 向 暮 山
舟 中 风 雨 寒
向 风 望 归 雁
暮 雨 归 家 难
滩 寒 帆 晚 还
```

**Across →**

> 孤舟向暮山  
> 舟中风雨寒  
> 向风望归雁  
> 暮雨归家难  
> 滩寒帆晚还  

**Down ↓**

> 孤舟向暮滩  
> 舟中风雨寒  
> 向风望归帆  
> 暮雨归家晚  
> 山寒雁难还  

**Rhyme:** 十一安 · an / ian / uan / üan · tones unrestricted.

→ 山 shān · 寒 hán · 雁 yàn · 难 nán · 还 huán

↓ 滩 tān · 寒 hán · 帆 fān · 晚 wǎn · 还 huán

**Why here:** Homeward difficulty and delay form a coherent situation in both readings. Watching geese becomes watching sails; the final difficulty shifts from human homecoming to the geese. One peer ranked it first for this more consequential closing contrast.

**Limit:** 滩寒帆晚还 is compressed and partly repeats the preceding cold and delay; 暮滩 is less settled diction than 暮山. The two poems retain very similar syntax.

**Reading:** 难 is nán, difficult; 还 is huán, return. A difficult homecoming followed by a late return is not a contradiction. 雁难还 does not mean return is impossible.

*Collection title; originally labelled Formal scaffold.*

### 3.《旧楼》 — Reserve

**Earlier entry** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
相 对 都 念 旧
看 图 说 旧 友
笑 问 楼 谁 守
如 故 不 嫌 旧
旧 友 修 旧 楼
```

**Across →**

> 相对都念旧  
> 看图说旧友  
> 笑问楼谁守  
> 如故不嫌旧  
> 旧友修旧楼  

**Down ↓**

> 相看笑如旧  
> 对图问故友  
> 都说楼不修  
> 念旧谁嫌旧  
> 旧友守旧楼  

**Rhyme:** 十欧 · ou / iou · tones unrestricted.

→ 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu · 守 shǒu · 旧 jiù · 楼 lóu

↓ 旧 jiù · 友 yǒu · 修 xiū · 旧 jiù · 楼 lóu

**Why here:** The old friends, image and building form an identifiable human situation. Repairing the building across versus guarding an unrepaired building down gives the form a factual consequence. It remains ahead of the new rhyming studies because its actions connect more securely.

**Limit:** 如故不嫌旧 has an indistinct attachment, and 旧 is overworked. The vertical poem says the building is not or will not be repaired; it does not explain why.

**Reading:** Do not turn 不修 into opposition to demolition, historically sensitive restoration, or secret repair. Those stories are not in the words.

*Collection title; originally labelled Old friends and an old building.*

### 4.《江边窗光》 — Workshop

**New this round** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
江 光 浮 西 墙
风 在 江 面 上
拂 水 帆 影 晃
南 面 船 临 窗
墙 上 晃 窗 光
```

**Across →**

> 江光浮西墙  
> 风在江面上  
> 拂水帆影晃  
> 南面船临窗  
> 墙上晃窗光  

**Down ↓**

> 江风拂南墙  
> 光在水面上  
> 浮江帆船晃  
> 西面影临窗  
> 墙上晃窗光  

**Rhyme:** 十三昂 · ang / iang / uang · tones unrestricted.

→ 墙 qiáng · 上 shàng · 晃 huàng · 窗 chuāng · 光 guāng

↓ 墙 qiáng · 上 shàng · 晃 huàng · 窗 chuāng · 光 guāng

**Why here:** The revised scene no longer needs an invented paper painting. Breeze, river light, sails, walls and windows belong to one physical setting. That stability puts it slightly above 寄知己 in the present ranking.

**Limit:** 浮江帆船晃 compresses a modifier awkwardly; 西面影临窗 leaves the shadow vague. Compass directions and repeated light descriptions still feel dictated by the crossings.

**Reading:** Across starts from river light on a wall and passes through breeze and sail reflection; down starts from wind against a wall and moves through water light and a swaying boat. These are rearranged observations, not sharply different emotional conclusions.

### 5.《寄知己》 — Workshop

**New this round** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
我 为 谁 写 诗
因 君 有 此 意
你 留 诗 待 寄
写 此 谁 人 知
此 笔 寄 知 己
```

**Across →**

> 我为谁写诗？  
> 因君有此意。  
> 你留诗待寄，  
> 写此谁人知？  
> 此笔寄知己。  

**Down ↓**

> 我因你写此，  
> 为君留此笔。  
> 谁有诗谁寄；  
> 写此待人知，  
> 诗意寄知己。  

**Rhyme:** 四衣 · i / -i · tones unrestricted.

→ 诗 shī · 意 yì · 寄 jì · 知 zhī · 己 jǐ

↓ 此 cǐ · 笔 bǐ · 寄 jì · 知 zhī · 己 jǐ

**Why here:** The final gift changes from the physical 此笔 to intangible 诗意. That is a worthwhile device, and both readings pass the formal checks. Independent review nevertheless found the whole expression less secure than its individual phrases.

**Limit:** 你留诗待寄 changes who holds and sends the poem without connecting the roles clearly. 谁有诗谁寄 is grammatical but abruptly becomes a general maxim. Do not invent reciprocal poetry exchanges or a mail-carrying arrangement to fill those gaps.

**Reading:** Across asks about purpose and recognition, ending with a pen sent to a confidant. Down states a cause for writing, keeps a pen for the addressee, and ends by sending poetic feeling. This remains an experiment, not a promoted finished pair.

**Revision record:** Author's final revision includes the parent's suggested 谁→待 at row 3 column 4; the author then independently replaced two characters to make the pen literal.

*Collection title.*

### 6.《姐姐》 — Workshop

**New this round** · Two rhyming poems · → ↓

```text
姐 姐 逗 我 乐
姐 姐 我 饿 了
笑 我 够 了 么
我 怕 了 姐 姐
饿 了 么 姐 姐
```

**Across →**

> 姐姐逗我乐。  
> 姐姐，我饿了。  
> 笑我，够了么？  
> 我怕了姐姐。  
> 饿了么，姐姐？  

**Down ↓**

> 姐姐笑我饿。  
> 姐姐，我怕了。  
> 逗我，够了么？  
> 我饿了，姐姐。  
> 乐了么，姐姐？  

**Rhyme:** 三鹅 · e / ie / üe · tones unrestricted.

→ 乐 lè · 了 le · 么 me · 姐 jiě · 姐 jiě

↓ 饿 è · 了 le · 么 me · 姐 jiě · 姐 jiě

**Rhyme limitation:** The displayed pinyin records the intended me. For interrogative 么 used like 吗, [汉典 lists ma](https://zdic.net/hans/%E4%B9%88), separately from its me uses. With ma, both third-line endings fall outside 三鹅. A deliberate me reading fits the pattern, but this is not a robust ordinary-pronunciation pass; it is excluded from strict selection.

**Why here:** The childlike complaint is more individual than many landscape grids. 逗我乐 becomes 笑我饿, and the vertical poem develops a recognizable protest. Its low strict-selection rank reflects an unresolved rhyme issue as well as the weaker horizontal ending, not a claim that colloquial writing is inferior.

**Limit:** 饿了么姐姐 suddenly asks about the sister’s hunger after the speaker’s hunger and fear. A charitable reader must not invent a tender reconciliation. Repeated 姐姐 and grammatical particles also carry much of the rhyme.

**Reading:** The final vertical question can sound reproachful: asking whether the sister has had her fun. Sarcasm is an interpretation; a specific abusive incident or reconciliation is not stated.

**Revision record:** Uses the author's proposed center-character repair 错→够. Original third lines were 笑我错了么 / 逗我错了么; all other characters unchanged.

Original pre-repair grid, retained for the record:

```text
姐姐逗我乐
姐姐我饿了
笑我错了么
我怕了姐姐
饿了么姐姐
```

*Collection title; author called this a near-miss.*

These groups have different constraints, so their ranks are separate. Same-poem squares are not claimed to work backwards; reversible grids are not claimed to work along arbitrary paths.

#### 1.《认人》 — Selected

**New this round** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
奶 奶 又 叫 妈
奶 奶 认 出 我
又 认 错 一 回
叫 出 一 声 来
妈 我 回 来 了
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 奶奶又叫“妈”。  
> 奶奶认出我，  
> 又认错一回。  
> 叫出一声来：  
> “妈，我回来了。”  

**Why here:** The neighboring 认出 and 认错 briefly offer recognition, then withdraw it; the closing quoted address makes that correction legible. Its specificity and concentrated turn narrowly outweigh 留一间’s smoother, more general reassurance.

**Limit:** 叫出一声来 is mainly a bridge into the quotation. 认出→认错 is an intentional correction or irony, not two simultaneously literal claims of correct identification.

**Reading:** The last line is read as the grandmother’s speech: 妈，我回来了. The poem does not establish a diagnosis, whether her mother is alive, or an unstated family history.

#### 2.《留一间》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
我 说 家 还 在
说 屋 里 有 人
家 里 留 一 间
还 有 一 人 等
在 人 间 等 你
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 我说家还在  
> 说屋里有人  
> 家里留一间  
> 还有一人等  
> 在人间等你  

**Why here:** Home narrows into a room and a waiting person before 人间 expands the final promise. The contextual omission of 房 in 留一间 is ordinary, not a repair. It remains an excellent companion to the more sharply particular 认人.

**Limit:** 说屋里有人 is mostly preparatory and repeats the reassurance. The scene is less individual than the top-ranked entry.

**Reading:** An available room and someone waiting are explicit. Death or permanent separation is not.

#### 3.《想听你笑》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
我 想 听 你 笑
想 起 你 和 我
听 你 骂 我 傻
你 和 我 一 样
笑 我 傻 样 儿
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 我想听你笑  
> 想起你和我  
> 听你骂我傻  
> 你和我一样  
> 笑我傻样儿  

**Why here:** The wish to hear laughter develops into remembered teasing. The mutuality of 你和我 suits the symmetric form, and the colloquial voice distinguishes it from the more conventional 候归.

**Limit:** 笑我傻样儿 is clipped and regionally marked. 想起你和我 is broad rather than concrete. These are limits of register and precision, not automatic grammatical failures.

**Reading:** Laughter supports an affectionate reading of 骂, without making it the only possible reading. 儿 occupies one written cell even if pronounced as erhua.

#### 4.《候归》 — Reserve

**Earlier entry** · One poem · → = ↓

```text
我 等 你 回 家
等 月 归 山 后
你 归 门 自 开
回 山 自 种 桃
家 后 开 桃 花
```

**Across → and down ↓ — identical**

> 我等你回家  
> 等月归山后  
> 你归门自开  
> 回山自种桃  
> 家后开桃花  

**Why here:** The opening welcome gains physical form in the opening door and flowers behind the house. It reads coherently, but is less individual and less tightly shaped than the higher-ranked same-poem squares.

**Limit:** 回山自种桃 moves from immediate return into a more general future of rural living. The planting and blooming need not happen on the same night.

**Reading:** The self-opening door is a small personification of welcome. No additional narrative is necessary.

#### 1.《留客》 — Selected

**New this round** · Four cardinal readings · → ↓ ← ↑

```text
山 深 归 客 远
寒 夜 人 留 客
归 人 等 人 归

```

**Across →**

> 山深，归客远。  
> 寒夜，人留客。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 客留人，夜寒。  
> 远客归深山。  

**Down ↓**

> 山寒，归客远。  
> 深夜，人留客。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 客留人，夜深。  
> 远客归寒山。  

**Reversed rows ←**

> 远客归深山。  
> 客留人，夜寒。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 寒夜，人留客。  
> 山深，归客远。  

**Reversed columns ↑**

> 远客归寒山。  
> 客留人，夜深。  
> 归人等人归。  
> 深夜，人留客。  
> 山寒，归客远。  

**Why here:** 归人等人归 is the strongest central line in this group: being homeward-bound does not end waiting. 人留客 and 客留人 reverse the relation between host and guest. All four orders remain intelligible.

**Limit:** The other person in 客留人 is unspecified. 深/寒 alters atmosphere more than fundamental meaning. Reversal reorders existing lines; there are nine distinct line strings, not twenty new ones.

**Reading:** The forward readings end with movement toward mountains; the reverse readings end with the returning traveler distant. 归人 does not establish that someone has already arrived.

#### 3.《未完的对话》 — Workshop

**Earlier entry** · Four cardinal readings · → ↓ ← ↑

```text
我 等 你 问 风
听 雨 听 山 问
你 问 不 听 我
等 风 问 雨 等
风 听 我 等 你
```

**Across →**

> 我等，你问风。  
> 听雨，听山，问。  
> 你问，不听我。  
> 等风，问雨，等。  
> 风听，我等你。  

**Down ↓**

> 我听，你等风。  
> 等雨，问风，听。  
> 你听，不问我。  
> 问山，听雨，等。  
> 风问，我等你。  

**Reversed rows ←**

> 风问，你等我。  
> 问山，听雨，听。  
> 我听，不问你。  
> 等雨，问风，等。  
> 你等，我听风。  

**Reversed columns ↑**

> 风等，你听我。  
> 听风，问雨，等。  
> 我问，不听你。  
> 等雨，听山，问。  
> 你等，我问风。  

**Why here:** As punctuated free verse, the changes between asking without listening and listening without asking genuinely alter the human relation. One peer narrowly preferred it to 长巷 for that dramatic effect. The present general-reading order gives fluency greater weight.

**Limit:** Sequences such as 听雨，听山，问 end in arranged-sounding one-word commands. The twenty distinct strings are not twenty equally strong lines. Punctuation is essential.

**Reading:** Reverse row 5 is 你等，我听风; reverse column 5 is 你等，我问风. Neither reading contains a shared 你我 subject.

#### 1.《入山》 — Selected

**Earlier entry** · Two poems, no rhyme requirement · → ↓

```text
微 风 入 竹 林
雨 轻 山 色 深
入 林 心 自 远
花 影 自 留 客
林 深 闲 客 居
```

**Across →**

> 微风入竹林  
> 雨轻山色深  
> 入林心自远  
> 花影自留客  
> 林深闲客居

> 微雨入花林  
> 风轻林影深

> 我听你说梦  
> 说你在梦里  
> 你说我非我  
> 仍旧梦相见  
> 在梦中见你  

**Why here:** Dreamed presence versus an actual meeting becomes a question about the dreamed person’s identity. That is a substantial formal idea worth retaining.

**Limit:** 仍旧梦相见 is conspicuously compressed, and dream/meeting vocabulary repeats without enough further movement.

**Reading:** Dreaming of meeting is the more defensible reading of 梦相见. Do not silently insert 在梦中 to claim that the written syntax is fully effortless.

## Review record

Five creators started with no inherited task conversation. Earlier poems and scores were not supplied during creation.
